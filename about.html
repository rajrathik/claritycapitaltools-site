About Clarity Capital Tools

Clarity Capital Tools is a collection of practical tools designed to make complex financial and operational questions easier to explore. It began with investment tools built around historical market data, and is expanding into free utilities for financial institutions.

Investing

Interactive tools for exploring portfolios, market history, risk and diversification — using what markets actually did rather than projections of what they might do.

Credit Union Utilities

Free utilities and open-source projects for technology, data and operational teams. Independently published, and not affiliated with or endorsed by any institution.

Our approach

  • Build practical tools.
  • Explain how they work.
  • Make assumptions visible.
  • Use real data where possible.
  • Avoid unnecessary complexity.

Where the investing data comes from, and what each simulation assumes, is documented in Data & Methodology.

What this is not. Not a brokerage. Not individualized financial advice. Not a prediction engine. The tools describe what happened in the past over the periods you select.
