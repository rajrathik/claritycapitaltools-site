About Clarity Capital Tools

Clarity Capital Tools is an independent collection of practical financial tools.

InvestSim uses historical market data to help investors explore portfolios, market cycles, risk and diversification without relying on forecasts.

We also publish selected open-source utilities for credit union technology and data teams.

The goal is simple: build useful tools, make their assumptions visible, and let people explore the results themselves.

Clarity Capital Tools is not a brokerage and does not provide individualized financial advice. Credit union utilities are published independently and are not sponsored or endorsed by any institution. Where the investing data comes from, and what each simulation assumes, is documented in Data & Methodology.
Historical data is provided for research and educational purposes. It is not individualized investment advice and is not a prediction of future performance.
